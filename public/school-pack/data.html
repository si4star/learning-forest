On the pupil's device

The browser keeps a copy of the pupil's forest so it opens without a connection, any answers waiting to be sent, and a few display flags (install prompt seen, reminder on). A class device also keeps a cookie that marks it as a class device for up to a year. Logging out clears the forest copy.

What we don't collect

  • No surnames, dates of birth, photos, addresses or pupil numbers are asked for.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. No third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Pupils can't message anyone or see other pupils' data.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords, recovery codes, QR keys, login tokens and device tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Logins are limited: 10 failed tries per account and 50 per network address in 15 minutes; 5 wrong picture tries per pupil.
  • Picture logins work only on class devices the teacher has set up.
  • Login cookies can't be read by scripts. Requests from other websites are refused.
  • A teacher can only see and change their own classes and pupils.

Deleting data

  • A pupil: the teacher removes them in the app. Their progress, answers, reminders and logins are deleted straight away.
  • A whole class at the end of the year: "Delete this class and all its pupils" deletes every pupil's data straight away and signs out the class devices.
  • A teacher account: email [contact email]. We delete the account and everything on it within [30] days.
  • Cloudflare database backups (Time Travel) are kept for [up to 30 days, confirm plan] and then overwritten.
