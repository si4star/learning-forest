What we store

For your data protection impact assessment. Last updated 6 October 2026.

Who is who

  • Your school is the controller. You decide which pupils use The Learning Forest and why.
  • The Tree Fella NE Ltd (trading as The Tree Fella®) is the processor. We hold the data only to run the app for you. ICO registration number ZA845692.
  • Lawful basis is for the school to decide. Schools usually rely on public task for teaching tools.

On the pupil's device

The browser keeps a copy of the pupil's forest so it opens without a connection, any answers waiting to be sent, and a few display flags (install prompt seen, reminder on). A class device also keeps a cookie that marks it as a class device for up to a year. Logging out clears the forest copy.

What we don't collect

  • No surnames, dates of birth, photos, addresses or pupil numbers are asked for.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. No third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Pupils can't message anyone or see other pupils' data.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords, recovery codes, QR keys, login tokens and device tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Logins are limited: 10 failed tries per account and 50 per network address in 15 minutes; 5 wrong picture tries per pupil.
  • Picture logins work only on class devices the teacher has set up.
  • Login cookies can't be read by scripts. Requests from other websites are refused.
  • A teacher can only see and change their own classes and pupils.

Deleting data

  • A pupil: the teacher removes them in the app. Their progress, answers, reminders and logins are deleted straight away.
  • A whole class at the end of the year: "Delete this class and all its pupils" deletes every pupil's data straight away and signs out the class devices.
  • A teacher account: "Delete my account" in the app deletes the account and everything on it straight away, after the teacher types their password. Or email learn@thetreefella.co.uk and we will do it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Automatically: a pupil who hasn't played for 12 months is deleted, with their progress, answers and logins. Their teacher sees a warning in the app from 11 months. A teacher account that hasn't been used for 12 months is deleted once it has no pupils left.
  • Cloudflare database backups (Time Travel) are kept for up to 30 days and then overwritten.

The Children's Code

The ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code applies to online services children are likely to use. How the app lines up with it:

  • Data minimisation: first names, nicknames or initials only. No surnames, dates of birth, photos, addresses, location or contact details.
  • High privacy by default: nothing is shared or public. Pupils can't message anyone or see other pupils. Daily reminders are off and can't be turned on by pupils.
  • No profiling for other purposes: answers are used only to choose a pupil's next questions and to show progress to their teacher or parent. No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No geolocation: the app never asks for or records location.
  • No data sharing: only the sub-processors above, to run the service.
  • No nudges to stay on: rounds are about 20 questions. A run of days isn't broken by missing one day a week.
  • Grown-ups' view: a pupil's teacher or parent can see their progress and tricky facts. The app shows nothing about what a pupil does outside it.
