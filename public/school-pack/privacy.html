Privacy notice

The Learning Forest is free learning for children, made by The Tree Fella NE Ltd. In Grow your times tables, children practise their times tables a few minutes a day and grow a forest as they learn. This notice covers the data it keeps. How does a tree work? needs no login and keeps no data [confirm once the page is added].

If your child uses it at school

Your child's school decides to use The Learning Forest and is in charge of your child's data (the "controller"). We look after the data for the school and only use it to run the app (the "processor"). Questions about your child's data, or requests to see or delete it, go to the school first. We will help them.

If you made the account yourself

We are the controller for your account and your children's data. We use it only to run the app, because you asked us to (our legitimate interest in providing the service you signed up for).

What we keep

  • The grown-up's email address and a scrambled (hashed) copy of their password.
  • Your child's first name or nickname, as typed by the grown-up, and their username.
  • Scrambled copies of your child's password, recovery code and QR code. In a class, the three pictures your child taps to log in.
  • Your child's answers: each question, the answer, whether it was right and how long it took. This is how the app picks the next questions.
  • Settings, such as the forest season and whether questions are read aloud.
  • If you turn on a daily reminder: your device's notification address and the time you chose.
  • Failed login attempts and the internet address they came from, for 24 hours, to stop people guessing passwords.

We never ask for surnames, dates of birth, photos or addresses. There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics. Children can't message anyone.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and the database. Database location: [D1 region].
  • Cloudflare Workers AI makes the read-aloud voice. It only receives app phrases like "6 times 7", never names.
  • If you turn on reminders, your phone's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) passes on an empty message to wake the app. The reminder is written on your device.

We don't sell or share data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until the account, class or child is deleted. Teachers can delete a child or a whole class at any time, and the data goes straight away. Failed logins are deleted after 24 hours. Database backups are overwritten within [30] days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your child's data, or object to how it is used. For school accounts, ask the school. Otherwise email us at [contact email].

If you're unhappy with how we've handled data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
