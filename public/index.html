Grow your times tables: how it works

The method is built on retrieval practice and spaced repetition, two well-evidenced ways to make facts stick.

🌱

A starting check

Pupils pick the tables they think they know and answer a few questions on each. Only the tables they really know are planted.

📅

Spaced repetition

A right answer sends a fact away for longer each time: same round, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, then 21 days. A wrong answer sends it back to a seed.

🧩

Easy facts first

Tables unlock in the order 10, 2, 5, 11, 3, 4, 9, 6, 8, 12, 7. New facts are worked out step by step from ones the pupil already knows.

🔁

One tree per fact pair

6 × 7 and 7 × 6 are the same tree, which turns 121 facts into 66. Grown trees are also asked as 6 × ? = 42 and 42 ÷ 6.

⏱️

Accuracy, then speed

No countdown. A tree can't grow past a sprout until it's answered within 6 seconds, the limit in the Year 4 Multiplication Tables Check.

📝

Practice check

Once most of the forest is grown, a 25-question practice check opens, set out like the Year 4 check.

Getting started in a class

One account per teacher. Setting up a class takes a few minutes.

  1. Make a teacher accountOpen Grow your times tables, tap "I'm a grown-up" and create an account with your email.
  2. Make a class and add pupilsUse first names or initials only. If two pupils share a name, add a surname initial, like "Bob A" and "Bob B".
  3. Print the Forest PassesEach pupil's card has three pictures to log in with in class, and a QR code to use at home.
  4. Set up the class devicesOn each tablet or computer, log in as the teacher and tap "Use this device for" your class. Pupils then tap their name and their three pictures.
  5. Little and oftenA few minutes a day. You can see how many trees each pupil has planted and when they last played.

At home too

Scanning the QR code on a pupil's Forest Pass logs them in on a phone or tablet at home. The forest is the same one they grow in class.

Picture logins only work on devices the teacher has set up for the class. Anywhere else, pupils use the QR code or their username and password.

End of the year

When a class moves on, delete the class and all its pupils' data in one go, or remove just the class and keep the pupils' forests on your account.

Ending a class's use never needs an email to us: the teacher deletes it in the app.
